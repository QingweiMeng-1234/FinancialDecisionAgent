Lead times, or the time taken between an initial order and its delivery, for some high-voltage transformers have increased to multiple years from around a year in 2020 and 2021.

Generator step-up transformer lead times surpassed 160 weeks by the first quarter of 2026, compared with an average of 143 weeks in 2024.

Utilities and developers have responded by purchasing equipment well in advance, refurbishing older transformers, asking customers to pre-pay for equipment that has long lead times, and diversifying sourcing. With large transformers for substations pushing past a three-year waiting time, Roseville Electric Utility is buying equipment for projects that it knows are coming five years in advance.
