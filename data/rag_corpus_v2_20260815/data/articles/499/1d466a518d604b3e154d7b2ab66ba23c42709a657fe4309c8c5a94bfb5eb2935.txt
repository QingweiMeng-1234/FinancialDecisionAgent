Customer support Whether customer support is effective is almost a case-by-case affair — sometimes it's quick and smooth, other times it can take a long time and get little resolved. It's usually best to approach carrier customer support expecting the worst and hoping for the best. Based on our experience, we've had most of our issues resolved on both Verizon and AT&T, but timing can vary. Both start you off with unhelpful automated support, but it's fairly easy to get in touch with a person. Verizon and AT&T also have convenient options for call-back scheduling, so you're not waiting on hold. Verizon and AT&T also have several physical locations, whether corporate or authorized third-party. That's helpful to get support from a human being for any issue. The Verizon and AT&T apps have everything you need for plan management. For Verizon's app, specifically, it's easy to navigate as long as you stick to the Account tab.
