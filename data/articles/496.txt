Prices are largely driven by the teams playing rather than the city itself, as the most popular teams can significantly raise ticket prices for a fixture. Brazil, Portugal, and Mexico games are trending the highest right now. At the time of writing, according to TicketData.com, San Francisco has the lowest average get-in price for group stage fixtures at $281. Atlanta is up next at $376, with Vancouver and Kansas also coming in a few bucks short of $400. On the other end of the scale, Mexico City has the highest average ticket price for group games at $1,465, followed by Miami ($1,271) and Guadalajara ($1,104).
